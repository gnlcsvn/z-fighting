z-fighting
z-fight!about

Z-fighting is a rendering error. When two surfaces lie in the same place, the depth buffer cannot decide which one is in front, and the picture breaks into a pattern of both.

This series provokes the error on purpose. Each mathematical surface is set against a copy of itself, turned by a fraction of a degree. The pictures on this site are computed live in your browser.

On paper they are drawn with a pen plotter, one pen for each body: blue horizontal strokes for the original, red vertical strokes for the copy.
